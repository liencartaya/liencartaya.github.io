About me

This website is currently under construction.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the Institute of Mathematics, Universidad de Talca, Chile.

My research interests include representation theory, reflection groups, non-commutative algebra, path algebras, and combinatorics.

My current work focuses on quaternionic reflection groups, zero fibers of quotient singularities, diagonal coinvariant rings, and related computational methods.

Research

I work in representation theory and invariant theory, with emphasis on reflection groups and non-commutative algebraic structures.

My doctoral research studies zero fiber rings associated with quaternionic reflection groups, combining theoretical tools with explicit computations.

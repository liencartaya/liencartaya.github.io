Ph.D. Candidate in Mathematics

Lien Cartaya

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Institute of Mathematics, Universidad de Talca, Chile. My research interests lie in representation theory, reflection groups, invariant theory, rational Cherednik algebras, non-commutative algebra, path algebras, combinatorics, and computational algebra.

My current work focuses on quaternionic reflection groups, zero-fiber rings of quotient singularities, diagonal coinvariant rings, and related computational methods.
